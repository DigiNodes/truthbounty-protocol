Protocol boundary

This step saves a local draft. Nothing is submitted on-chain until the final authorization step.

Approved journey coverage

The implementation must preserve every state transition represented below.

1 · Claim

Capture the exact assertion, category and claimant acknowledgement as a local draft.

2 · Evidence

Record retrievable sources, provenance and integrity metadata without uploading secrets.

3 · Funding

Quote stake, fees and allowance from pinned interfaces; never infer affordability.

4 · Review

Show a deterministic summary, warnings and the final transaction payload.

5 · Authorize

Request wallet approval and distinguish rejected, reverted, replaced and dropped states.

6 · Reconcile

Separate chain confirmation from indexer projection and offer recovery when they diverge.
