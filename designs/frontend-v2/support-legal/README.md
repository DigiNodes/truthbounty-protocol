# TruthBounty V2 — Support and Legal Screen Collection

Batch 6 completes the screen-design authority with public documentation, help, service transparency, contact, support, security, privacy, terms, disclosures, legal-version handling and system support states. It inherits the approved brand, foundation, public/claimant, verifier/dispute and privileged authorities.

## Boards

1. Documentation home
2. Documentation article
3. Help and troubleshooting
4. Service-status overview
5. Incident detail and history
6. Contact and support entry
7. Support-request form
8. Security and vulnerability reporting
9. Privacy notice
10. Terms of use
11. Protocol-risk disclosures
12. Legal-document version and consent state
13. Not found, unavailable and maintenance states

Run `node generate-support-legal-boards.mjs` to regenerate the SVG collection. Boards use a fixed 1600 × 1000 viewBox and the approved TruthBounty visual tokens.

## Publication boundary

- The privacy and terms boards are review structures, not approved legal copy.
- Missing operator, jurisdiction, contact, retention, processor, age and liability decisions remain visibly unresolved.
- No security bounty, response SLA, uptime guarantee, support endpoint or subscription service is invented.
- Public status must use freshness-labelled observations and cannot expose protected diagnostics.
- Support cannot edit claims, votes, disputes, settlements, balances or canonical contract state.
- No board activates contributor work or authorizes the `Stellar Wave` label.
