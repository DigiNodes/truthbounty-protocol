# Responsive and Accessibility Policy

## Baseline

All golden journeys must be completable with keyboard and assistive technology at supported viewport sizes. Accessibility is part of acceptance, not a post-wave polish task.

## Maintainer requirements

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA for user-facing flows.
- Preserve logical DOM, heading, landmark, and focus order across layouts.
- Provide accessible names, descriptions, errors, and status announcements.
- Never convey protocol or transaction status by color alone.
- Support 200% zoom and reflow without loss of action or information.
- Respect reduced motion and sufficient touch target sizing.
- Keep tables understandable on narrow screens through approved responsive patterns.
- Place focus predictably after navigation, validation failure, dialogs, and async completion.
- Announce transaction changes without repeatedly overwhelming users.

## Contributor evidence boundary

Contributors may produce the per-screen acceptance matrix and automated/manual test mapping. Any exception, role-specific tradeoff, or change to these baseline requirements requires maintainer approval.
